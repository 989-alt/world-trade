# 1. Scene
A lively river port in Vietnam: wooden boats loaded with fruit, a long assembly factory with open doors showing sewing and electronics lines, rows of scooters, palm trees, conical hats hanging at a stall, misty green hills behind. Center foreground, two stylized 3D characters face the viewer, three-quarter body: a Vietnamese factory supervisor in a yellow vest and conical hat holding a clipboard, and a young Korean woman customs officer with dark hair in a neat bun, in a navy uniform with a plain navy peaked cap bearing only a small gold anchor emblem, holding a clipboard, both smiling warmly. The lower third of the canvas is a clean pier surface kept clear for a dialogue panel, and the top eighth is clear sky. Polished stylized 3D illustration in the same casual mobile game style as the approved concept: smooth clay-like shading, rounded forms, soft gradients, glossy highlights, big friendly character proportions. Every sign, banner, badge, flag and label is a plain color surface.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level wide shot with a mild low angle on the characters, background softly defocused, characters crisp.
# 3. Lighting
Bright warm sunlight from the right, soft bounce fill from the ground, gentle rim light separating the characters from the background.

Smooth stylized 3D render look, rounded edges, subtle clay-like surface, polished game-art finish.
