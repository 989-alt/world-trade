# 1. Scene
A sprite sheet of twenty miniature 3D trade-goods icons arranged in a grid of four rows and five columns with equal cells and generous even spacing, each icon centered in its cell, on a flat solid magenta background. Row one: a black oil barrel, a blue gas cylinder, a dark graphite lump, a green nickel ore chunk, a stack of steel coils. Row two: a sack of rice, a sugar cube pile, a bunch of green grapes, a cacao pod, a flat pack of dried seaweed. Row three: a red fuel jerrycan of refined fuel, a glossy green battery, a small white car, a microchip, a smartphone. Row four: a pink cosmetics bottle, a film clapperboard, a play-button screen, a vaccine vial, a small friendly robot. Polished stylized 3D illustration in the same casual mobile game style as the approved concept: smooth clay-like shading, rounded forms, soft gradients, glossy highlights, big friendly character proportions. Every sign, banner, badge, flag and label is a plain color surface.
# 2. Camera
Straight-on orthographic view, every icon the same visual size, aligned to the grid.
# 3. Lighting
Soft studio light from the upper left, glossy highlights, soft contact shadow under each icon.
# 4. Color grading
Bright friendly palette: sand #F7E7B4, sea #3FA7E6, navy #1B2A41, orange accent #F2994A, gold #F2C14E. Around 5400K, clean saturated.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Smooth stylized 3D render look, rounded edges, subtle clay-like surface, polished game-art finish.
