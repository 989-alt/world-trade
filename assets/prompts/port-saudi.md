# 1. Scene
A harbor town on the Arabian Gulf coast: rounded sandstone buildings with golden domes and arched windows, date palms, a wooden pier in the foreground, a cute red container ship with stacked colored containers and a small wooden dhow at the dock, round white oil tanks and a refinery tower on the far shore under a clear sky. Center foreground, two stylized 3D characters face the viewer, three-quarter body: an Arab merchant in a white thobe and red-checked headscarf holding a ledger, and a young Korean woman customs officer with dark hair in a neat bun, in a navy uniform with a plain navy peaked cap bearing only a small gold anchor emblem, holding a clipboard, both smiling warmly. The lower third of the canvas is a clean pier surface kept clear for a dialogue panel, and the top eighth is clear sky. Polished stylized 3D illustration in the same casual mobile game style as the approved concept: smooth clay-like shading, rounded forms, soft gradients, glossy highlights, big friendly character proportions. Every sign, banner, badge, flag and label is a plain color surface.
