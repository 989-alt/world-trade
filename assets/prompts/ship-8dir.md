# 1. Scene
A sprite sheet of one cute rounded cargo ship seen from directly above, with a navy hull, a white bridge at the stern and stacked red, blue and yellow containers, drawn in eight headings in two rows of four equal cells on a flat solid magenta background: top row facing north, northeast, east, southeast; bottom row facing south, southwest, west, northwest. Same ship, same scale in every cell, a short white wake trailing behind each. Polished stylized 3D illustration in the same casual mobile game style as the approved concept: smooth clay-like shading, rounded forms, soft gradients, glossy highlights, big friendly character proportions. Every sign, banner, badge, flag and label is a plain color surface.
# 2. Camera
Straight-on top-down orthographic view, ship centered in each cell.
# 3. Lighting
Soft sunlight from the upper left, gentle highlights on the containers, small soft shadow on the hull side.
# 4. Color grading
