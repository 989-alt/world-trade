# 1. Scene
A busy modern Chinese container port: tall orange gantry cranes, stacks of colored containers, a mountain of dark graphite ore in an open yard, steel coils on the quay, a glass skyline with a pearl-shaped tower behind, red lanterns strung along the pier. Center foreground, two stylized 3D characters face the viewer, three-quarter body: a Chinese trader in a smart grey jacket holding a tablet, and a young Korean woman customs officer with dark hair in a neat bun, in a navy uniform with a plain navy peaked cap bearing only a small gold anchor emblem, holding a clipboard, both smiling warmly. The lower third of the canvas is a clean pier surface kept clear for a dialogue panel, and the top eighth is clear sky. Polished stylized 3D illustration in the same casual mobile game style as the approved concept: smooth clay-like shading, rounded forms, soft gradients, glossy highlights, big friendly character proportions. Every sign, banner, badge, flag and label is a plain color surface.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level wide shot with a mild low angle on the characters, background softly defocused, characters crisp.
# 3. Lighting
Bright warm sunlight from the right, soft bounce fill from the ground, gentle rim light separating the characters from the background.

Bright friendly palette: sand #F7E7B4, sea #3FA7E6, navy #1B2A41, orange accent #F2994A, gold #F2C14E. Around 5400K, clean saturated.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Smooth stylized 3D render look, rounded edges, subtle clay-like surface, polished game-art finish.
